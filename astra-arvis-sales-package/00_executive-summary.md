## The Bottom Line

**2026 is the most financially favorable year in a decade to purchase capital imaging and navigation equipment.** The One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA), signed July 4, 2025, permanently restored 100% first-year bonus depreciation — meaning a $300,000 Astra Arvis system purchased today can be fully deducted in Year 1, generating an immediate tax offset of up to **$111,000 at the 37% individual bracket** or **$63,000 at the 21% corporate rate**. There is no phase-down, no sunset, and no dollar cap under bonus depreciation.

At the same time, CMS added **560 new orthopedic and surgical procedures** to the ASC-covered procedure list for 2026 — including revision joint arthroplasty and complex spine fusions that were inpatient-only just 12 months ago. The market these facilities can now capture has expanded materially, and facilities without navigation capability cannot access the highest-acuity, highest-revenue cases in that expanded list.

The convergence of these two forces — maximum tax incentive and maximum addressable procedure market — creates a uniquely compelling case for purchasing now.

## The Five Numbers That Move the Needle

**1. $126,000** — Real after-tax cost of a $200,000 Astra Arvis system for a surgeon-owner in the 37% bracket using 100% bonus depreciation. The tax system covers $74,000 of the purchase price in Year 1.

**2. ~7 months** — Estimated payback period on a navigation system that enables 50 additional total joint cases per year at orthopedic ASC average contribution margins. ($375,000 incremental EBITDA ÷ ~$200,000 net after-tax equipment cost.)

**3. $3.375M** — Estimated increase in ASC enterprise value from $375,000 in incremental annual EBITDA, at the orthopedic ASC EBITDA multiple of 7–10x. A surgeon with 25% ownership gains approximately $843,000 in equity value.

**4. 9.0% vs. 31.8%** — Rate of implant malalignment outliers (>3°) in navigated vs. conventional TKA. More than 70% reduction in outliers — the clinical foundation for the commercial payer outcomes narrative.

**5. June 30, 2026** — Hard deadline for Section 179D energy-efficient construction deductions on facility build-outs (construction must begin by this date). First-time ASC developers building now should not miss this.

## Why Act in 2026 — Not 2027

- **100% bonus depreciation is now permanent** — but deferring the purchase defers the tax savings. At an 8% discount rate, a $74,000 deduction taken in 2027 instead of 2026 costs approximately $5,900 in lost present value. Every year deferred is another year of foregone tax shield.
- **CMS expanded ASC procedure eligibility** — facilities equipped today capture the revenue from newly eligible complex cases from Day 1. Competitors equipped a year from now capture it a year later.
- **Orthopedic navigation market is growing at 13.9% CAGR** — equipment costs will rise. Purchase price today reflects current market; acquisition delay carries both price and opportunity cost.
- **Surgeon recruitment depends on technology availability** — younger orthopedic surgeons trained on navigated/robotic platforms prefer facilities with compatible technology. First-mover ASCs in a market lock in surgeon alignment; followers recruit from what remains.
