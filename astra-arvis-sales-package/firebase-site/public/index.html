Objection Handling

The Four Objections Every Rep Encounters

"Not in this year's budget."
Most $100–500K revenue-generating items fall within CFO-level authorization — no board cycle. Frame as revenue retention. At $5K blended differential/case, $250K system is covered by retaining 50 cases. Ask: "What's your authorization threshold?"
"We need better ROI data."
One variable dominates: how many cases does your committed surgeon group bring? 4 TJAs/month = $480K/year. $126K after-tax cost → payback under 4 months on 4 cases/month. Offer 30 minutes building a custom utilization model.
"We're evaluating [Competitor X]."
Evaluate: total cost of ownership including service and upgrade obligations; procedure categories enabled vs. your case mix; OR time at steady state. Offer a surgeon-to-surgeon reference call — more useful than any demo.
"Waiting on reimbursement landscape."
No payer pays a facility premium for navigation-assisted TJA — extremity codes (0054T/0055T) are bundled. Don't sell a billing premium that doesn't exist. The case is: volume (navigation enables the highest-fee categories), quality (avoidance of revisions under bundled payment), and competitive position.

Six Key OBBBA Provisions

  • 100% bonus depreciation — permanent, IRC §168(k)
  • Section 179: $2,560,000 limit (2026, inflation-adjusted)
  • QBI deduction: 23% of qualified pass-through income (up from 20%)

  • Estate/Gift: $15M individual / $30M couple permanent exemption

Key Distinction: Bonus depreciation acquisition date is January 19, 2025. Section 179 effective date is January 1, 2025. When discussing contract timing, use the bonus depreciation date. Finance with a loan (not a lease) — ownership is required to claim the deduction.

"Is this facility for-profit or not-for-profit?"
For-profit = the full tax story applies. Bonus depreciation, Section 179, after-tax cost — all of it lands. Non-profit = different conversation (volume + reimbursement focus).
✓ For-profit answer → flag it
02
"When is your next capital planning cycle or CapEx review?"
An active or upcoming cycle means there's a window. Capital decisions have timing — if they're in cycle now, urgency is real. If they're 6 months out, you have time to build the case.
✓ Active cycle → flag it
03
"Have you seen the 2026 CMS changes to MSK procedures in the ASC setting?"
Most administrators haven't fully absorbed the new list. If they haven't — that's your opening. If they have — they're already thinking about case expansion, which means equipment ROI is top of mind.
✓ Awareness gap → flag it
You're not the CFO's tax advisor. Your job is to find the signal and get the right people in the room.
