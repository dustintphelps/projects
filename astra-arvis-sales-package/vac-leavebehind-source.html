What Changed in CY 2026 (CMS Final Rule)

  • 285 MSK procedures removed from IPO list

  • New Level 7 Musculoskeletal APC for complex revisions and multilevel fusions

Critical Reimbursement Clarification

  • CMS does NOT pay a facility premium for navigation-assisted TJA
  • Extremity navigation CPTs 0054T/0055T are bundled under comprehensive APCs — not separately billable for TKA/THA
  • Exception: Spine navigation CPTs 61781–61783 ARE separately billable under HOPD OPPS — generate incremental billing on top of the spine fusion facility fee
  • The financial case for joint navigation is volume, quality, and competitive position — not a billing premium
Market direction: Sg2 Health Consultants projects 51% of total joints will be performed outpatient by 2026. The site-of-service shift is not a prediction — it is already the dominant trend in orthopedic surgery delivery.

Competitive Position Reality

At 20.4% of TKA volume using robotic assistance — with sixfold growth in six years — this is no longer early-adopter technology. Facilities without navigation are increasingly asked by surgeons and patients why they don't have it.

69% of patients surveyed believe robotic-assisted surgery produces fewer complications and better outcomes (2021 patient survey). Consumer awareness has crossed the threshold where navigation-capable facilities have a measurable advantage in patient-choice markets.

Surgeon Recruitment: The Training Cohort Effect

Orthopedic surgery graduates completing residency and fellowship from 2018 onward trained on robotic and navigation platforms as a standard part of their education. These surgeons actively seek facilities with compatible technology as a condition of employment or privileges, and in documented cases have stated: "I will never do another joint replacement without robotics technology." This creates a structural recruitment advantage for navigation-equipped facilities that is not replicable by compensation alone.

The U.S. faces a projected shortage of 5,080 orthopedic surgeons by 2025 (AAOS projections). In a supply-constrained market, facilities offering navigation recruit from a larger eligible pool. Facilities without it recruit from what remains.

VAC Committee Reference: Objections & Responses
Decision Support
Common ObjectionEvidence-Based Response
"Not in this year's capital budget" Most $100–500K revenue-generating purchases fall within CFO or VP-level authorization — no board cycle required. Frame as revenue retention: at $5,000 blended differential per case, a $250K system is covered by retaining 50 cases. Request mid-year approval with a one-page retention analysis using your facility's actual TKA volume.
"We need better ROI data before committing" One variable dominates the ROI: committed cases per month from your surgeon group. 4 additional TJAs/month = $480K/year in revenue. At $126K after-tax cost, payback is under 4 months. Request a 30-minute utilization modeling session with your specific case volume and payer mix data.
"Navigation slows down OR throughput" The learning curve is real but short: 9–20 cases to time neutrality depending on the system. After the learning curve, OR times are equivalent to or faster than conventional. "It slows us down" is valid for the first 1–2 months only and is not a durable argument against adoption.
"We're evaluating a competitor system" A comparison worth doing thoroughly. Evaluate total cost of ownership (service contract terms, upgrade cycle obligations), specific procedure categories each system enables vs. your case mix, and OR setup time at steady state. Surgeon-to-surgeon reference calls with current users of each system provide the most actionable comparison.
"There's no reimbursement premium for navigation" Correct — and any vendor claiming otherwise is overstating the case. CMS bundles extremity navigation CPTs (0054T/0055T) under comprehensive APCs. The financial case is: (1) navigation enables access to the highest-facility-fee procedure categories; (2) quality outcomes data drives payer contracting leverage; (3) competitive position at 20.4% robotic TKA market share. Spine navigation (61781–61783) IS separately billable.
"Let's wait and see how the reimbursement landscape develops" The 2026 CMS rule has already restructured the landscape. 285 procedures came off the IPO list. 560 new ASC-eligible procedures are effective now. Waiting does not change these structural shifts — it only delays the facility's ability to perform and bill for the new procedure categories that require navigation.
